# Great Britain Study 02 — Types of British Racing

## Conclusion

British racing is organised around two broad forms: **Flat** and **Jump**. The study established the hierarchy first and then measured the observed programme using the governed corrected race-type field.

Across **111,634 Great Britain races** from **1 January 2015 through 27 May 2026**:

- **70,208 races (62.9%)** were Flat;
- **41,426 races (37.1%)** were Jump.

The balance changed strongly through the year. Flat racing dominated the summer programme, while Jump racing formed a much larger share in winter. August averaged about **87.7% Flat**, while Jump was the majority in February, March, November and December.

Using the analytical course-date grouping from Study 01, the programme contained **9,786 Flat-only groups**, **6,068 Jump-only groups** and **11 mixed groups** after the verified source corrections were applied.

The full notebook contains the breakdown of the three Jump subtypes and the source-governance work behind the corrected figures.

## Limits

The source period ends on 27 May 2026, so 2026 is incomplete. The year 2020 is treated as exceptional rather than as a normal seasonal baseline. The course-date grouping is analytical and is not an official meeting definition.
