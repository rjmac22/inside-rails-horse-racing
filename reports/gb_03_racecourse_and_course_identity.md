# Great Britain Study 03 — British Racecourse and Course Identity

Study 03 established that source course labels, governed racecourse identities and individual course/track identities are different concepts.

The accepted model is:

**racecourse → course/track → time-bounded characteristics**

The final baseline contains **61 governed racecourse identities**, **65 source-label mappings**, **90 course/track inventory rows**, **86 stable course/track identities** and **7 unresolved governance rows**.

The work is integrated into accepted Database v4. Unresolved questions remain explicitly unresolved rather than being filled by assumption.
