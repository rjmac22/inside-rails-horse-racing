# Inside Rails Reports

This folder is the reader-friendly entry point for completed Inside Rails research.

The files here are deliberately written as normal Markdown reports so they are easy to read in the GitHub mobile app. The notebooks, closeout records, governed evidence and database release documents remain the technical source of truth.

## Great Britain studies

All four studies below are fully closed.

1. [Study 01 — Governance and structure](gb_01_governance_and_structure.md)  
   What does the British racing programme look like in scale, weekly rhythm and seasonality?

2. [Study 02 — Types of British racing](gb_02_types_of_british_racing.md)  
   How do Flat and Jump racing fit together, and how much of each do we observe?

3. [Study 03 — British racecourse and course identity](gb_03_racecourse_and_course_identity.md)  
   What is a British racecourse, and why is a racecourse not always the same thing as a course or track?

4. [Study 04 — Race meetings and fixtures](gb_04_race_meetings_and_fixtures.md)  
   What do British racing sources mean by meeting and fixture, and can `date + racecourse` identify one?

In GitHub Mobile, open `rjmac22/inside-rails-horse-racing`, select the `main` branch, open `reports`, then tap any report above. Markdown should render as a normal formatted page.

## Evidence policy

These reports summarise what the research established; they are not substitutes for the underlying evidence. Each Great Britain report links back to its notebook and, where available, its formal closeout record. Findings are kept separate from unresolved questions and from stronger claims the evidence does not support.
